## Problem

A small online store still has to get the hard parts right: totals that match what is charged, stock that can't be oversold, and shipping costs before checkout.

## My role

I'm the main developer of Expressus Café, a DeepSpace project.

## Approach

- **Storefront** built with Next.js.
- **Checkout integrity.** Totals and stock changes run inside transactional database functions, so an order either completes in full or not at all.
- **Guest carts.** You can shop without an account; the cart lives on an HTTP-only cookie.
- **Payments and shipping.** Stripe Checkout with signed webhooks, and shipping quotes from Skydropx.
